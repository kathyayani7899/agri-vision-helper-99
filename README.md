# AgriSmart Assist

Build an AI solution called “AgriVision AI” — a smart assistant platform for farmers and rural communities. The app should help users: * Detect crop diseases using AI image analysis * Predict weather-related farming risks * Recommend fertilizers and crop treatments * Monitor soil and water conditions * Improve sustainability and reduce waste * Provide rural advisory support in simple language ⸻ Core Features 1. AI Crop Disease Detection (Computer Vision) Users can: * Upload a photo of a crop leaf * Capture images directly from mobile camera * Detect: * plant diseases * pest attacks * nutrient deficiencies * unhealthy crops The AI should: * Analyze the image using a trained computer vision model * Highlight affected regions visually * Return: * disease name * confidence score * possible causes * treatment suggestions * prevention tips Supported crops: * Rice * Wheat * Tomato * Cotton * Maize * Chili * Potato ⸻ 2. Smart Farming Advisory Assistant Create an AI chatbot assistant that: * Answers farming questions in simple language * Supports regional/rural-friendly communication * Gives recommendations based on: * crop type * soil condition * season * weather * location Examples: * “Why are my tomato leaves turning yellow?” * “Which fertilizer is best after heavy rain?” * “When should I irrigate my rice field?” The assistant should: * Explain clearly * Avoid overly technical wording * Encourage consulting agricultural experts for severe issues ⸻ 3. Weather & Yield Prediction Use AI prediction workflows to: * Forecast: * rainfall * drought risk * heat stress * flood risk Provide: * irrigation recommendations * sowing time suggestions * harvesting alerts Also create: * crop yield prediction dashboard * seasonal risk analysis * sustainability scoring ⸻ 4. Soil Health Analyzer Users can enter: * soil pH * moisture * nitrogen * phosphorus * potassium levels AI should: * assess soil quality * recommend fertilizers * suggest crop suitability * detect over-fertilization risks Add visual indicators: * healthy * moderate * critical ⸻ 5. Sustainability & Environmental Impact Module The app should: * estimate water usage * track fertilizer overuse * calculate sustainability score * suggest eco-friendly farming methods Include: * rainwater harvesting tips * organic alternatives * crop rotation suggestions * carbon footprint awareness
